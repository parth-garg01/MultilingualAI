PRD: Project "LinguaFlow" (Working Title)

Vision: To bridge the gap between global languages while maintaining the emotional and cultural nuances of human speech.

1. Executive Summary

A real-time, multimodal AI application that provides low-latency speech-to-speech translation for English, Hindi, and Tamil. The system focuses on Emotional Intelligence (EQ) and Cultural Localization, ensuring that sarcasm, idioms, and humor are accurately conveyed rather than translated literally.

2. Target Audience

  • International Travelers: Navigating local nuances in India or English-speaking regions.
  • Business Professionals: Conducting cross-cultural meetings where tone and rapport matter.
  • Healthcare/Support Services: Clear communication in high-stakes, emotional situations.

3. Key Features & Functional Requirements

A. Real-Time Multimodal Pipeline

  • Streaming ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition): Convert spoken Hindi, Tamil, or English into text with sub-second latency.
  • Simultaneous Translation: Begin translating sentences before the speaker has finished (incremental processing).
  • Neural TTS (Text-to-Speech): Generate natural-sounding voice output in the target language.

B. Emotional & Contextual Intelligence

  • Sentiment Analysis: Detect the speaker’s tone (angry, happy, sarcastic, urgent).
  • Prosody Mapping: The output voice should mimic the input's pitch, volume, and speed to reflect the original emotion.
  • Idiomatic Mapping: Instead of translating "It's raining cats and dogs" literally, the AI finds the equivalent Tamil or Hindi idiom.

4. Technical Stack (Proposed)

  • Core Models: * Whisper (OpenAI) or SeamlessM4T (Meta): For robust multilingual speech-to-text.
    • LLMs (GPT-4o or Gemini 1.5 Pro): To handle the "cultural bridge"—interpreting sarcasm and idioms.
  • Emotion Detection: Custom-trained CNNs or transformers for audio feature analysis (pitch/timbre).
  • Deployment: WebSockets for the simultaneous "always-listening" feel.

5. User Flow

  1. User A selects input (Hindi) and output (English).
  2. User A taps "Speak" and begins talking into the phone.
  3. The AI analyzes the audio stream in two parallel tracks:
    • Track 1: Semantic meaning (The words).
    • Track 2: Emotional metadata (The vibe).
  4. The AI generates a translated script that swaps local slang for culturally relevant target-language slang.
  5. User B hears the translation in real-time with a voice that sounds appropriately concerned, excited, or humorous.

6. Success Metrics (KPIs)

  • BLEU/METEOR Score: Measuring translation accuracy.
  • Latency: Must be under 1.5 seconds for a natural conversation flow.
  • Human Eval: A "Nuance Score" given by native speakers on how well sarcasm and idioms were handled.

7. Potential Challenges

  • Code-Switching: Users often mix Hindi and English ("Hinglish"). The AI must handle mixed-language input.
  • Ambient Noise: Filtering out background noise on a phone while retaining the speaker's emotional tone.
  • Sarcasm Detection: Sarcasm is notoriously difficult for AI; it requires deep integration between the text meaning and the audio pitch.
